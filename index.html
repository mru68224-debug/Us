Secret Prettiest Cutest Us

We’ve walked a path that’s anything but smooth.
We’ve clashed, we’ve hurt, we’ve tested each other’s patience,
and sometimes, in the heat of it all,
I wondered if we could survive the storms we made ourselves.

And yet… here we are. Still.
Still choosing each other even when it’s hard,
still finding the little threads that tie us together,
still realizing, in quiet moments,
that life without you is impossible to imagine.

Be my Valentine today, tomorrow, and every day we get to stay in this little world we’ve built.
