Secret Prettiest Cutest Us

We’ve walked a path that’s anything but smooth.
We’ve clashed, we’ve hurt, we’ve tested each other’s patience,
and sometimes, in the heat of it all,
I wondered if we could survive the storms we made ourselves.

And yet… here we are. Still.
Still choosing each other even when it’s hard,
still finding the little threads that tie us together,
still realizing, in quiet moments,
that life without you is impossible to imagine.

We’ve made mistakes we’ve said things we wish we hadn’t,
we’ve carried silences heavy enough to break hearts.
But through it all, we’ve kept coming back,
because some truths don’t need words:
you and I, we belong in each other’s presence.

So here’s to us imperfect, fiery, tender, alive.
Here’s to staying, even when it’s difficult,
to forgiving, even when it’s messy,
to laughing, to crying, to being exactly who we are together.

Let’s keep this story alive,
not because it’s easy, but because it’s ours,
because every beat of my heart
reminds me that I cannot and do not want to
exist without yours nearby.

Be my Valentine today, tomorrow, and every day we get to stay in this little world we’ve built.
